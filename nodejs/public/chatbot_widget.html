GPSphere Chatbot
GPSphere is the official digital system for GPS UTM members and students. It manages registrations, events, approvals, and communication.
Go to the Register tab → fill in your details → wait for admin approval. Once approved, you become a GPS member.
TAC (Time Authentication Code) is a security code sent to your email. Enter your password → click Login → check email → enter TAC.
Login as a member → go to your dashboard → view events → click Apply on your desired role → wait for admin approval.
Every event has different roles. Examples include Director, Secretary, Technical Crew, Helpers, and more depending on event structure.
You can contact the GPS committee through social media or by visiting the GPS room at UTM.
