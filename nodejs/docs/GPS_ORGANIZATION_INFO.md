# GPS - Gerakan Pengguna Siswa (Student Consumer Movement)

## 📖 About the Organization

**Gerakan Pengguna Siswa (GPS)** or **Student Consumer Movement** is an educational NGO (Non-Governmental Organization) dedicated to empowering students with essential consumer knowledge and skills.

## 🎯 Mission

To educate and empower students with comprehensive consumer knowledge, enabling them to make informed, ethical, and responsible purchasing decisions while protecting their rights and the environment.

## 🌟 Vision

A generation of smart, ethical, and responsible student consumers who understand their rights, make wise decisions, and contribute to a fair and sustainable marketplace.

## 🎓 Core Objectives

### 1. **Consumer Rights Education**
- Teaching students about their fundamental rights as consumers
- Right to safety
- Right to information
- Right to choice
- Right to be heard
- Right to redress
- Right to consumer education
- Right to a healthy environment

### 2. **Smart Decision Making**
- Developing critical thinking skills for evaluating products and services
- Learning to compare prices, quality, and value
- Understanding marketing tactics and advertisements
- Avoiding impulsive purchasing decisions
- Reading and understanding product labels
- Recognizing quality vs. marketing hype

### 3. **Responsible Consumption**
- Understanding environmental impact of consumption
- Promoting sustainable and eco-friendly choices
- Reducing waste and promoting recycling
- Supporting ethical businesses
- Understanding the lifecycle of products
- Making socially responsible purchasing decisions

### 4. **Fraud Prevention & Protection**
- Recognizing common consumer frauds and scams
- Identifying deceptive marketing practices
- Understanding online shopping safety
- Protecting personal and financial information
- Knowing how to report fraud
- Understanding consumer protection laws

### 5. **Financial Literacy**
- Basic budgeting for students
- Understanding value for money
- Saving strategies
- Avoiding debt traps
- Understanding credit and loans
- Smart spending habits

- **Consumer Rights Workshops**: Interactive sessions on fundamental consumer rights
- **Financial Literacy Classes**: Teaching budgeting and money management
- **Product Comparison Skills**: Learning to evaluate and compare products
- **Sustainable Consumption**: Understanding environmental impact
- **Digital Consumer Safety**: Online shopping and privacy protection

### Practical Activities
- **Role-Playing Scenarios**: Simulating real-world consumer situations
- **Market Visits**: Practical shopping exercises with guided learning
- **Label Reading Sessions**: Understanding nutritional and product information
- **Budget Challenges**: Hands-on budgeting activities
- **Consumer Complaint Workshops**: Learning to exercise consumer rights

### Community Engagement
- **Peer Education**: Students teaching other students
- **Consumer Awareness Campaigns**: Spreading knowledge in schools and communities
- **Newsletter & Publications**: Sharing consumer tips and information
- **Social Media Education**: Using digital platforms to educate
- **Parent Workshops**: Involving families in consumer education

### Special Programs
- **Anti-Fraud Campaigns**: Raising awareness about scams
- **Sustainable Living Workshops**: Promoting eco-friendly choices
- **Rights Advocacy**: Teaching students to stand up for their rights

- **Competitions & Quizzes**: Making learning fun and engaging

## 👥 Target Audience

### Primary Beneficiaries
- **Students** (ages 12-25): The main focus group
- **School Students**: Secondary and high school students
- **College/University Students**: Young adults entering independent consumer life

### Secondary Beneficiaries
- **Teachers & Educators**: Partner in delivering consumer education
- **Parents & Families**: Supporting student learning at home
- **Community Members**: Broader impact through student advocacy

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

After participating in GPS programs, students will be able to:

1. **Know Their Rights**: Understand and exercise their consumer rights
2. **Make Informed Decisions**: Evaluate products and services critically
3. **Budget Wisely**: Manage their money and avoid wasteful spending
4. **Recognize Frauds**: Identify and avoid consumer scams
5. **Consume Responsibly**: Make ethical and sustainable choices
6. **Advocate**: Teach others and advocate for consumer protection
7. **Seek Redress**: Know how to complain and seek solutions
8. **Think Critically**: Analyze marketing and make independent decisions
